Record which numbers are emphasized, how comparisons are phrased, whether baseline methods are named, and whether results are grouped by experiment.

## Ending Pattern

Record whether the article ends with significance, limitations, future outlook, or a short conceptual sentence.

## Reuse Plan

Write a short plan for the new article: title candidates, section order, where to insert each figure, and which reference-style phrases to imitate without copying.
